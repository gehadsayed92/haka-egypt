## Slides 5–7 · Concept 1 — FUEL. by Protocol

**Core idea:** The first coffee bar engineered like a supplement stack — every drink has a dose, a function, and a claim.

**Why it wins in Egypt:**
- Cairo's gym + wellness wave (Gold's, O2, Nike Run Club, pilates boom).
- Zero functional-coffee operator currently serving that demand.
- Protein lattes are proven globally (Blank Street); Egypt has no branded owner yet.
- Premium pricing justified by function, not just coffee — breaks the EGP 90 ceiling.

**Signature Menu (7):**
1. The 30g Protein Latte *(viral)*
2. Cortado Creatine
3. Adaptogen Cold Foam Americano *(high margin)*
4. Collagen Cloud Matcha
5. Electrolyte Espresso Tonic
6. Nootropic Nitro *(experiential)*
7. The Pre-Workout Shot (triple ristretto, beetroot, ginger, cayenne)

**Customer Journey:** Entry → neon "Dosage Board" → pick goal first (Focus / Recover / Burn / Build) → barista prints macro receipt → served in clinical vial + glass → photo moment at LED Stack Wall.

**Design:** Brushed stainless, white micro-cement, amber apothecary shelving, black rubber floor accents, chrome nitro tap wall, 4000K spotlights. *Apothecary × Equinox × Blank Street.*

**Economics:**
- Gross margin **78–82%** (ingredients EGP 8–14, priced EGP 130–180).
- Add-on stacks lift ticket **+25%**.
- Scalable to **10 units in 24 months**.

**Risks:** ingredient import complexity · claim-regulation phrasing · needs nutritionist credibility partner.

## Slides 8–10 · Concept 2 — TURBA (تُربة)

**Core idea:** A coffee house that reinvents Egyptian pantry flavors — date, tahini, hibiscus, dom, carob, mastic, orange blossom — as a modern specialty menu.

**Why it wins in Egypt:**
- No operator owns Egypt's own flavor story (Saudi has Camel Step, UAE has clones).
- Pistachio/tahini already TikTok-proven — localization = cultural pride + export narrative.
- Hard differentiation from the generic Italian-Scandinavian template.

**Signature Menu (7):**
1. Karkadeh Cold Foam Espresso Tonic *(viral — ruby/black gradient)*
2. Tahini Date Latte *(hero)*
3. Dom & Cream Cold Brew *(high margin)*
4. Orange Blossom Cortado
5. Mastic Matcha *(experiential)*
6. Carob-Cocoa Mocha
7. The Qahwa Flight (Turkish / espresso / cold-brew w/ cardamom ice) *(experiential, shareable)*

**Customer Journey:** Entry → wall of raw ingredient jars → Flavor Compass menu (Earthy / Floral / Roasted / Tart) → open prep bar → served on hand-thrown Fayoum pottery → Origin Card with farm provenance.

**Design:** Travertine, oxidized copper, raw linen, Fayoum ceramics, limewash in terracotta and bone, subtle Nubian-pattern textile accent, 2700K pendants. *Aesop × Cairo Design Week × Koffee Mameya.*

**Revenue Logic:**
- Local sourcing cuts COGS ~**40%** vs imported syrups.
- Avg ticket **EGP 110–150**.
- **Two revenue streams**: café + retail line (bottled syrups, blended beans, export).

**Risks:** conservative-customer alienation (keep 2 classic anchors) · heritage-kitsch drift (design must stay ruthlessly modern) · artisanal supply consistency.

## Slides 11–13 · Concept 3 — HAND / POUR

**Core idea:** Cairo's first 8-seat coffee omakase bar — a 25-minute curated flight of single-origin pours, paired with scent, sound, and dessert. Reservation-only.

**Why it wins in Egypt:**
- HNW / luxury class over-consumes Nobu, Zuma, Sachi — no coffee equivalent exists.
- Scarcity = status: 8 seats × 4 sessions/day = booked-out + waitlist culture.
- Breaks the EGP 300/cup ceiling via experience pricing.

**The Flight (5 courses):**
1. Espresso tonic with geisha micro-shot *(opener)*
2. Ethiopia Yirgacheffe V60 — poured at your seat *(experiential)*
3. Nitro cold brew · cascara foam · olive oil float *(viral)*
4. Nile Geisha — Panama Geisha, date-orange blossom reduction, oat cream *(highest margin)*
5. Mastic affogato · single-origin bean ice cream
- Takeaway: engraved brass bean tin.

**Customer Journey:** App reservation → concierge arrival → 8-seat onyx bar → barista narrates origins → 5 drinks across 25 min with paired scents + soundscape → closing gift → hero moment at signature pour (overhead-lit).

**Design:** 40m², dark walnut, backlit honey onyx bar, single brass pendant per seat, charcoal limewash, indigo Japanese textile accent, hidden speakeasy door. *Koffee Mameya × Bar Leone × The Aviary.*

**Economics:**
- Flight priced **EGP 650–850/head**.
- 8 seats × 4 sessions × EGP 700 = **EGP 22,400/day** from 32 covers.
- Smallest footprint, highest yield-per-sqm in the portfolio.
- Secondary: bean retail + merch.

**Risks:** thin addressable market (~80 bookings/week required) · world-class-barista dependency · booking-only format must read as exclusivity, not barrier.

## Slide 14 — Final Recommendation

| Rank | Concept | Role | Launch |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **FUEL. by Protocol** | Commercial engine — volume + margin + scale | Q3 2026 |
| **2** | **TURBA** | Cultural flagship + retail + export optionality | Q1 2027 |
| **3** | **HAND / POUR** | Prestige halo adjunct — 8 seats inside TURBA | Q3 2027 |

Lead with **FUEL.** to bank the fastest ROI and build the operational muscle.
Layer **TURBA** to secure cultural defensibility and a retail line that outlives the café footprint.
Add **HAND / POUR** last as the halo that repositions the whole portfolio as luxury.
