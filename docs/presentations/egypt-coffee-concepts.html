Market Opportunity

Egypt's specialty market is growing — but copying itself.

  • 15%+ YoY growth in Egyptian specialty coffee spend since 2022.
  • Gen Z & Millennials now spend 3× more on experiential F&B vs. 2022.
  • ~80% of premium cafés are Italian-Scandinavian clones — commoditized.
  • Zero national operator owns functional, local-modern, or ritual categories.
  • Ticket ceiling in Cairo still ~EGP 90 — breakable with the right positioning.

Visual: Cairo map heatmap of café density (Zamalek / New Cairo / Sheikh Zayed saturation) + white-space callouts.

02 / 14
Global Trends · 2025–2026

What's actually winning globally.

  • Functional coffee — protein, creatine, adaptogens, nootropics (Blank Street, Erewhon).
  • Cold & textural — nitro, cold foam, layered espresso tonics dominate hot-climate volume.
  • Experimental flavors — pistachio, tahini, miso, pandan, ube, olive oil, date.
  • Multi-sensory rituals — omakase bars, hand-drip theater (% Arabica, Koffee Mameya).
  • Sustainability as premium — direct-trade storytelling justifies margin, not CSR.

Visual: 5-panel grid — Blank Street protein latte, % Arabica Kyoto, Oleato close-up, Koffee Mameya bar, Erewhon tonic.

Concept 02 · Cultural Flagship

TURBA · تُربة

Egypt's pantry, reimagined as specialty coffee.

  • Date, tahini, hibiscus, dom, carob, mastic, orange blossom — as a modern coffee language.
  • No global operator can copy this. Cultural moat.
  • Opens a second revenue stream: bottled syrups, blended beans, retail line.
  • Export optionality: GCC + Egyptian diaspora in EU.
  • Breaks free of the Italian-Scandinavian template flooding Cairo.

Visual: interior render — travertine counter, oxidized copper shelving, Fayoum ceramics, wall of jars with hibiscus / dates / mastic / carob, 2700K pendants.
